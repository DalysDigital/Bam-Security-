01 / ABOUT BAM

Professional Security for a Safer Meghalaya

Trained. Disciplined. Professional.

Established in 2004, the Bouncers Association of Meghalaya (BAM) is a non-profit organization representing and regulating the bouncer industry in Meghalaya.

BAM provides disciplined security personnel and crowd-management support with a focus on preparedness, professionalism, conflict resolution, emergency response and responsible conduct.

06 / OUR STORY

About Us

Security for Events, Concerts & Large Gatherings

BAM was founded with a vision to elevate the standard of security services in the region. Starting with a small group of dedicated professionals, the organization built a reputation around training, discipline and commitment.

Today, BAM continues to expand its services while keeping the same focus: professional people, clear responsibilities and a secure environment for clients and the public.

VISION

Professional Event Security
